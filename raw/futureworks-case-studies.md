# FutureWorks Case Studies (scraped from future.works/work/)

Source: https://future.works/work/ — pulled 2026-11-03. Figures are FutureWorks' own marketing claims as published; baselines/measurement not stated unless noted. 12 of 51 listed case studies fetched (the featured ones). Base URL pattern: https://future.works/case-studies/[slug]/

## Strongest proof for industrial / manufacturing / supply chain ICP

### NextPower (solar trackers, energy) — /nextracker/
- Challenge: rapidly expanding service + product line; shipping/tracking inefficient and opaque; new software line needed C-suite buy-in.
- Did: business review, built NxOs shipping & tracking platform, interactive prototypes for C-suite, logistics ops interface, composable interface system, tablet/mobile for field.
- Timeline: first cycle 12 weeks; continued in 3-month cycles (2+ years).
- Results: 20,000+ work hours reduced per year; product launched with customers in 6 months; 40 PV projects added in year 1.
- Quote: Steve Berriatua, VP Digital Transformation — "Future Works has been instrumental in NextPowers digital transformation."

### Philips North America (industrial supply chain) — /philips/
- Challenge: apply AI to P&L-driving parts of the business; measurable working-capital and freight outcomes in first cycle.
- Did: value & maturity mapping, Approved Value Baseline locked in Week 2 by Philips Finance, 12-week cycles, senior experts orchestrating AI agents on proprietary harnesses.
- Results: headline "2-6x return in 12 weeks"; Cycle 1 working-capital and freight outcomes signed off by Philips Finance; expanded into AI transformation roadmap across integrated supply chain and global services.
- Caveat: supporting figures gated (access key required). No public quote.

### Partsimony (manufacturing supply chain platform) — /partsimony/
- Challenge: position to multi-billion-dollar supply chain enterprises; antiquated manufacturing engagement process.
- Did: AI strategy, visual identity, product design/dev; separate Manufacturer and Hardware-company interfaces (sourcing, timeline tracking, order mgmt, CAD analysis).
- Results: 10x increase in enterprise pipeline.
- Quote: Rich Mokuolu, Founder — "Future Works brought our brand and product experience to a whole new level."

### JLL (commercial real estate) — /jll/
- Did: rapid diagnostic of global leasing ops, board-approved AI strategy, built Lease Intelligence Platform (document processing, secure search, analytics).
- Results: 70% faster lease processing.
- Caveat: no timeline/quote; full brief gated.
